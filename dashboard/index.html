What this board does not say

  • A protocol on this board records no token in the snapshot. That is DefiLlama’s record, not proof it has none — a token may exist that its record does not carry.
  • Implied value is not a prediction of whether or when a token is issued, or what it would trade at. It is the board’s own reading of the protocol’s record.
  • Farm plans are generic steps derived from each protocol’s category, chains, size and age. They are not financial advice, and no step asks you to pay to claim anything.
